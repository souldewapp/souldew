Community rules

souldew lets you add friends, show a profile and play SoulLinks together. These rules apply to everything other players can see: your display name, profile, status, showcase, run names and invites – in the app on the iPhone and on the computer. By signing in to a souldew account you agree to them.

Zero tolerance

There is no place in souldew for objectionable content or abusive users. Not allowed:

  • harassment, bullying, threats or hate against anyone – for their origin, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or anything else;
  • sexual, violent or otherwise offensive names, images or texts;
  • pretending to be someone else, including the souldew team;
  • spam, scams, links to cheats for sale or to pirated games, and selling or trading accounts;
  • sharing other people’s personal data.

Report and block

On every profile, friend, request and invite you can report a player or block them. Blocking takes effect at once: they can no longer see your profile, send you requests or invite you. Reports go to the souldew team.

What we do

We look at every report within 24 hours. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and the player who posted it is suspended or banned – depending on what happened, without warning. Banned players can’t create a new account with the same Apple ID or Discord account. Reports and moderation records are kept for 180 days, as described in the privacy policy.

Questions

Ask on the souldew Discord or see Support. For legal matters, the contact details are in the Impressum.
