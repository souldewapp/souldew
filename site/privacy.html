Privacy policy

This page explains what the souldew website and the souldew app send, to whom and why. The German version, Datenschutzerklärung, says the same; if the two ever differ, the German one applies.

1. Who is responsible

2. The website

Hosting. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. To deliver the pages GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and information about your browser (server log files). We have no access to these logs. Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. More: GitHub’s privacy statement.

No cookies, no analytics. The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking and embeds no third-party content. Fonts and pictures come from the same server as the page.

The latest version. So the download buttons point straight at the newest files, your browser asks GitHub’s public API (api.github.com) for the latest release when the page loads; this sends your IP address to GitHub. The answer (version number, file names, sizes) is kept in your browser’s session storage for up to 30 minutes and never leaves your browser. Without JavaScript the buttons lead to the release page on GitHub. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is a working download.

The thank-you wall. When you scroll near the “Thank you” section, your browser asks souldew’s accounts service (accounts.souldew.app, run by us on Cloudflare – see section 3.6) for the public list of supporters. The request carries no cookie and no referrer; like every request it carries your IP address, which Cloudflare processes to deliver it. The list holds only what supporters chose to show (see section 3.6). If the request fails, the page shows a static text instead. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is thanking the people who support souldew.

Links. Links to GitHub, Discord, Instagram and Buy Me a Coffee lead to those providers. Data goes to them only when you click a link; their privacy policies apply there.

3. The souldew app

souldew runs on your computer (the iPhone app: section 4). Your games, save files and runs stay there. The app has no analytics. It contacts the following services – most of them only when you use the feature in question.

3.1 Update check

At start and every four hours the app downloads latest.json from the latest souldew release on GitHub (github.com, a service of GitHub, Inc., see section 2) and checks its signature. If there is a new version, the installer is downloaded from the same GitHub release. GitHub sees your IP address and the request; nothing else is sent.

3.2 Playing together (SoulLink, Play together, link sessions, partner video)

The apps of a run find each other through public Nostr relays and then connect directly to each other (WebRTC). Where a direct connection isn’t possible, a public MQTT broker is used as a fallback for run data, and link sessions (trades, battles, raids, Shared World) can be relayed through Cloudflare’s TURN service; the short-lived TURN credentials come from souldew’s server at api.souldew.app. What travels is encrypted: tracker data, the buttons pressed during a link, and – if you watch each other – your partners’ game picture. Not encrypted is only a short online notice per player – the name you play under in that run, that you are online, and the time – which the brokers keep as a stored message under an address derived from the run that reveals nothing about it. Never your games or save files. Relays and the TURN service see the IP addresses of the connection.

3.3 Anonymous “playing now” counter

So the souldew Discord can show how many people are playing right now, the app sends – only while a game actually runs in souldew’s own players – a short message once a minute to our server at api.souldew.app (if that address can’t be reached at all, to the older address of the same server at workers.dev). It contains only:

  • a random id made when the game starts – new for every game, never stored on your computer, derived from nothing,
  • the system (Game Boy, Game Boy Advance, Nintendo DS or Nintendo 3DS),
  • the mode (solo, Nuzlocke, SoulLink or Play together) and
  • the app version.

When the game closes, “stopped” is sent once. No name, account, game title, save, run data or file is sent; offline, nothing is sent and nothing is sent later.

Storage: for each running game the server keeps only these details and the time of the last message; they are deleted with “stopped”, at the latest a few minutes after the last message. Only totals (games running per system and mode, one value per five minutes) are kept, for 90 days. We don’t store your IP address and don’t log the messages; Cloudflare processes it to deliver the request and to limit abuse (an in-memory limit per address, nothing stored). No profiles are made. Anyone can read the totals at api.souldew.app/presence.

Provider: the server runs on Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (Cloudflare Workers and D1). Data may be transferred to the USA; Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More: Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is showing how active the community is and planning capacity.

Switching it off: Settings › Online › “Count me in ‘playing now’ (anonymous)” (up to version 2.8: Settings › General), the welcome tour or the Windows installer – on by default. Off applies at once. The details: docs/PRIVACY.md.

Daily usage count (the same switch). From version 3.0.1, once a day – the first time the app runs that day – it also tells the same server that souldew was used, so we can see how many people use it, which versions and systems, and whether people come back. The message contains only: a random value made for that day only (kept in memory and never reused, so a retry isn’t counted twice), the app version, the operating system and processor type, the calendar week souldew was first used on this computer, on how many of the last 7 and 28 days it ran, and whether today is the first day. To work this out, the app keeps the first-use date and the dates (no times) it ran in the last 28 days in a small file on your computer; nothing is recorded while the switch is off. The server keeps only totals per day – how many apps, how many new, by version, system and processor, and per first-use week how many were active – for 400 days; each day’s random value is deleted after two days. No IP address is stored, no profiles are made, and one day’s message can’t be linked to another’s. Legal basis as above (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); our interest also includes knowing which versions and systems need our support. Switching off “Count me in” stops this too.

3.4 Sharing to Discord (Live Runs and webhooks)

Only if you set it up yourself. With Live Runs you link souldew to your Discord account once with a code from /live connect in the souldew Discord; souldew’s server at api.souldew.app answers with a token that is stored in its own file in your data folder. From then on the app sends the run events you choose to share, through our server, to Discord. If you enter a Discord webhook for a run, the app posts that run’s events there directly. In both cases Discord Inc. receives what you share; Discord’s privacy policy applies there.

3.5 souldew account (optional)

Without an account the app works as before and sends none of the following.

Sign-in. “Sign in with Discord” opens your browser at accounts.souldew.app, which sends you to Discord to confirm. souldew asks Discord only for the scope identify and receives your Discord user id, user name, display name and profile picture – never your password or email address. Your sign-in is kept in your computer’s keychain.

What the accounts service stores: your account (Discord id, name and picture as above, your souldew handle and friend code), the profile you set up (banner, frame, nameplate, title, accent colour, showcase, status line, privacy settings), your friends, friend requests, blocks and run invites, your sign-ins per device, and – only if you switch on “Show my runs on my profile” – summary numbers of your runs (no saves, no ROM data, no file paths). While the app runs and you are signed in, it tells the service that you are online and, depending on your settings, what you are playing; your friends see this according to your privacy settings, and “appear offline” hides it.

Find a SoulLink partner: only if you use it. What you type in the form (game, how many partners, language and time zone, when you play, rules) goes through the accounts service to our Discord bot, which posts it publicly in #find-a-partner on the souldew Discord under your Discord account – just like the bot’s own form there. Discord’s privacy policy applies to the post; you close it on Discord, and it closes by itself after 7 days.

Your choices: who sees your profile and your status is set in Settings › Account. There you can also export your data as a file, sign out devices and delete your account; deleting it removes your profile, friends, invites, online status and sign-ins at once. Reports about you or made by you, and moderation records, are kept for 180 days under a keyed hash of your Discord id (not reversible without our secret key) and then deleted.

Provider and legal basis: the accounts service is run by us on Cloudflare (Workers, D1, R2; see section 3.3 for Cloudflare). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – providing the account and friends features you ask for.

3.6 souldew+ and the thank-you wall

souldew+ memberships are paid on Buy Me a Coffee; their privacy policy applies to the payment. Buy Me a Coffee tells our accounts service about the membership (tier and period, with the email you used there). We don’t keep that email in readable form: the service stores a keyed hash of it, so the membership can be linked to your account when you enter the same email in Settings › Account – or through /supporter claim in the souldew Discord. The souldew+ looks are then downloaded from the accounts service, encrypted for your account.

7-day trial: when you start the free souldew+ trial, the service stores a keyed hash of your Discord id (not reversible without our secret key) with the trial’s start and end. This record is kept for good – also after you delete your account – so each Discord account gets one trial. For the same reason the service remembers, under the same keyed hash, that a Discord account has had souldew+ or Legend: the trial is only for accounts that never had it. Nothing is paid and nothing renews. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the trial you ask for; Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for keeping the hash, our legitimate interest being that a trial can’t be repeated.

Roles on the souldew Discord: if you are on the souldew Discord server, our bot gives you the role that matches your tier (Supporter, souldew+ – also during a trial – or Legend), visible to the server’s members like any role, and takes it away when the tier ends. For that the accounts service tells the bot, our own server, which Discord ids have which tier – every ten minutes, nothing else. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is showing supporters on our server the role they paid for.

Thank-you wall: the app’s Thank you page and the website’s “Thank you” section show supporters by name only if they say so: a new supporter is asked once (“Yes, show me” / “No thanks”), and Settings › Account › Privacy › “Show my name on the Thank you page” switches it on or off at any time. Shown are the name, the tier, since when and – where the supporter has one – their profile picture. Everyone else is only counted in the totals.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the membership; Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time) for appearing on the thank-you wall.

3.7 Pictures

Pokémon sprites and game covers are loaded from public GitHub repositories (PokeAPI, libretro-thumbnails) and cached on your computer. Friends’ profile pictures are fetched by the app through its local server. The iPhone app downloads no pictures of Pokémon or games (section 4.1).

4. The souldew app for iPhone

The iPhone app is souldew made for the phone: the built-in emulator, the tracker, SoulLink, trades and battles. Your games, saves, save states and runs stay on your iPhone, inside the app; like other app data they are part of your iPhone’s backup. The app contains no games and downloads none. It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn’t send the anonymous “playing now” counter or the daily usage count (section 3.3) – the iPhone app has neither. Updates come from the App Store. What Apple itself processes – downloads, purchases, and the crash reports and usage figures you may choose in the iOS settings to share with app developers, which Apple then passes on to us – is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

4.1 Pictures

The pictures of the Pokémon are read from your own game files on your iPhone and kept there; DS games show their own icon from the game file. The app loads no pictures from the internet – except profile pictures, if you use an account (section 4.3).

4.2 Playing together (SoulLink, trades and battles, Shared World)

Only when you open a SoulLink run. The apps of a run find each other through public Nostr relays (the default list of the Trystero library, for example nos.lol and relay.nostr.dev.br) and then connect directly to each other (WebRTC). To find a direct path, the app asks public STUN servers of Google (stun.l.google.com) and Cloudflare (stun.cloudflare.com) for its public address. Where no direct connection is possible, the run’s data goes through public MQTT brokers instead: broker.emqx.io (EMQ Technologies), broker.hivemq.com (HiveMQ) and test.mosquitto.org (Eclipse Foundation). The iPhone app doesn’t use souldew’s TURN service (section 3.2).

What travels is end-to-end encrypted (AES-GCM, with a key derived from the run’s secret in the invite, which only the players’ devices know): the run’s tracker data (catches, deaths, the team, rules, the log); your status for your partners (the battle you are in, where you are in the game); during a trade or battle the buttons pressed and the state of the running game, directly between the devices; in Shared World your position in the game, waves and emotes. Relays, STUN servers and brokers can’t read it; they see the IP addresses of the connection. The brokers keep the run’s latest encrypted state as a stored (“retained”) message, so a partner who comes online later gets it. Not encrypted is only a short online notice per player – the name you play under in that run, that you are online, and the time – which the brokers keep too, under an address derived from the run that reveals nothing about it. Your game files are never sent. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – the link you set up with your partners.

4.3 souldew account (optional)

Without an account the app works completely and sends none of this. The account is the same as on the computer (section 3.5), with these differences:

  • Sign in with Apple: Apple’s own sign-in sheet. souldew asks Apple for no name and no email address, so it receives neither – not even a “Hide My Email” address. Apple gives souldew a user id that is valid for souldew only. The accounts service stores that id with your account and, encrypted, the token Apple issues for the sign-in – only to revoke your Apple sign-in at Apple when you delete the account or remove Apple from it, as Apple requires.
  • Sign in with Discord: a sign-in sheet of iOS opens accounts.souldew.app and Discord; as on the computer, souldew gets only your Discord id, name and picture. You can link Discord to an account made with Apple, and Apple to one made with Discord.
  • Your sign-in is kept in your iPhone’s keychain, on this device only. In your list of signed-in devices the iPhone appears as “souldew on iPhone” – never with your device’s name.
  • Online status: only while the app is open and in front – about once a minute, that you are online and, depending on your privacy settings, which game and mode you are playing (the place in the game only if you allow it). When the app goes to the background it reports you offline and sends nothing more; with “Appear offline” or “Hide me completely” it sends no status at all.
  • Profile pictures of you and other players are loaded from Discord’s picture server (cdn.discordapp.com) or through the accounts service; Discord sees your IP address when a picture is loaded from it.
  • Friends, invites, reports and blocking work as on the computer.
  • Deleting your account: More › souldew account › Delete account. Your account, profile, friends, requests, invites, online status and sign-ins are deleted at once; your Apple sign-in is revoked at Apple. Reports about you or made by you, and moderation records, are kept for 180 days under a keyed hash of your Apple or Discord id (not reversible without our secret key) and then deleted; if the same Apple ID or Discord account signs up again within that time, they are attached to the new account. Your runs and saves on the iPhone are not touched.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – the account and friends features you ask for; for keeping reports and moderation records after a deletion, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest being to stop abuse from simply starting over.

4.4 souldew+ on the iPhone

On the iPhone souldew+ is an App Store subscription. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account. When you subscribe, the app adds an id to the purchase that is derived from your souldew account (a hash – not your name, not your Apple ID). The app sends the purchase, as signed by Apple, to the accounts service, which checks Apple’s signature and unlocks souldew+ for your souldew account – on the iPhone and on the computers you sign in on. Apple also tells the service when the subscription renews, ends, is refunded or revoked (App Store Server Notifications).

The service stores with your account the subscription’s original transaction id, the product and tier, whether it is a test purchase, its status, the end of the period and of any grace period, and whether it renews automatically – no receipts, no prices, no payment details. The record is deleted 90 days after access ends. When you delete your account the subscription is unlinked, not deleted, so a subscription that still runs can be restored into a new account. Manage or cancel it in iOS (Settings › your name › Subscriptions); deleting your souldew account doesn’t cancel it. The souldew+ looks are downloaded from the accounts service, encrypted for your account. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

4.5 Links

Links in the app – this privacy policy, the legal notice, souldew’s Discord, GitHub and Apple’s terms of use – open in Safari. Data goes to those providers only when you tap a link; their privacy policies apply there.
