Your partners,
in your world.

Partners on the same map walk around in your game – with their trainer sprite from your own ROM, a name tag in their colour and their lead Pokémon one step behind.

  • Walk up, press A. Trade, Battle or Wave, straight from the menu.
  • Hearts, “!”, “?” and GG. Little pixel bubbles over your head, on every screen that sees you.
  • Rock-paper-scissors. Sealed hands, swapped only once both have picked.
  • Watch their battles. Crossed swords over their head – “Watch” opens their game live.
  • Catches and tombstones. Only what the run records, where it happened.
  • Gifts and “Come to me”. Hand over an item from your bag, or point everyone to where you are.

In Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver, Crystal, every Gen 3 game, Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver. Drawn over your game – nothing is written into it.

      Good
      to know.

      Find SoulLink partners, get help and share your runs on the souldew Discord.

      Is souldew still free?

      Yes – free and open source under the GNU GPL v3.0 or later, with no ads. Everything that plays is free: emulators, SoulLink, Nuzlocke tracking, trades, battles, raids, Shared World and friends. souldew+ only adds looks.

      Do I need an account?

      No. An account is optional – you sign in with Discord when you want a profile and a friends list. souldew never sees your Discord password and only gets your name and picture. Without an account everything works exactly as before.

      Do all players need 3.0?

      To trade, battle, raid and walk around together, yes. A partner still on 2.8 keeps syncing the run – catches, deaths, the log, the rules – and is told to update. Linking without a restart works from the fast player; a game in the browser emulator joins the link from its last in-game save.

      Is a link safe for my run?

      Before every link souldew keeps your save. If the linked games ever fall out of step, both go back to exactly where they were before the link. Nothing a link session shows counts for the run – a battle there never files a death – and the run’s rules decide whether trades and link battles are allowed.

      Does souldew include any games?

      No. You play your own, legally obtained ROMs. souldew recognises them by header and checksum, and when it searches your computer it only reads file names and headers.

      I already support souldew – how do I get souldew+?

      Link your Buy Me a Coffee membership in Settings › Account with the email you used there, or with /supporter claim in the souldew Discord.

      Something went wrong – what should I send?

      Right after it happens, use Save diagnostics in the app’s help and attach the file to a bug report. It contains what the app read and decided – no ROM, save or invite data.
