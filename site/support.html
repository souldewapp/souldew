Support

Help with souldew on your iPhone and on your computer – where to ask, how to report a bug, and answers to the questions that come up most.

Get help

The souldew Discord is the quickest way: discord.gg/kvVDCyzdpQ. Ask in #help, where the community and the team answer. For something private, open a ticket in #tickets – “Help with the app”, “Bug report (private)” or “Something else” – or type /ticket anywhere on the server. A ticket is a channel only you and the souldew team can see. The bot suggests a first answer right away; “Talk to the team” brings in a person.

From the app. On the iPhone: More › About souldew › Help and feedback opens the Discord, Report a problem opens GitHub. On the computer: Settings › About › Report a bug.

Bug reports go to GitHub (public) or into a private ticket. Say which device and souldew version you use, which game, and what you did right before. On the computer, Settings › About › Save diagnostics gives you a file to attach – it holds what souldew read from the game and decided, no ROM, save or invite.

Questions

Does souldew come with games?

No. souldew contains no games and downloads none. Play ROMs you made yourself from cartridges you own. On the iPhone: Library › Add ROM – .gb, .gbc, .gba or .nds, from Files, iCloud Drive or a USB drive. souldew copies the file into the app. On the computer, add your ROMs in the library; there 3DS games work too.

Where are my saves?

On the iPhone, the game’s own save (saving in the game’s menu) is kept by itself; save states are extra snapshots on top. Both stay inside the app on your iPhone and are part of your iPhone’s backup (iCloud or computer). Deleting a run deletes its save and save states; the ROM stays in the library. Deleting the app deletes everything in it. On the computer, saves are in souldew’s data folder; in the library, open a game › Import a save file … to bring one from another emulator, and Save game file gives you a plain .sav that other emulators read.

How do I manage or cancel souldew+ on my iPhone?

On the iPhone, souldew+ is an App Store subscription. Change or cancel it in the iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or with Manage subscription on the souldew+ page in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period, or it renews. Apple handles payment and refunds. Deleting your souldew account does not cancel the subscription. A membership bought outside the App Store is managed where you bought it.

I paid, but souldew+ isn’t there

souldew+ belongs to your souldew account. Sign in with the same account you used when you subscribed (More › souldew account), then open the souldew+ page and tap Restore purchases. An App Store subscription can belong to one souldew account only – if it is linked to another one, the app says so. Still missing? Open a ticket on the Discord.

Sign in with Apple or with Discord?

An account is optional – everything else works without one. On the iPhone you can sign in with Apple or with Discord; on the computer with Discord. With Apple, souldew asks for no name and no email address. Want the same account on your computer? Link Discord to it on the iPhone (More › souldew account), then sign in with Discord on the computer.

How do I delete my account?

On the iPhone: More › souldew account › Delete account. On the computer: Settings › Account. Your profile, friends, friend requests, invites and sign-ins are deleted at once, on every device; it can’t be undone. Your runs and saves on your devices are not touched. An App Store subscription keeps running until you cancel it in the iPhone’s settings. What is kept for a while, and why: privacy policy.

Is souldew made by Nintendo or The Pokémon Company?

No. souldew is an independent fan project, free and open source. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures Inc. or The Pokémon Company.
